The aim is simple: contribute good questions quickly, get them academically verified, and reuse them efficiently while preparing question papers.

1. Who Can Use This Module?

  1. Linked teaching staff can use the Question Bank and Question Paper Builder.
  2. A teacher may also have delegated authority such as Subject Coordinator; that review area appears in addition to the teacher workspace.
  3. The Principal is also treated as a teacher. A Principal/Admin account that is linked to a teaching identity can use the normal teacher Question Bank and Paper Builder and can separately open the Principal / QB Admin area for oversight.
  4. Non-teaching staff do not receive the teacher Question Bank workspace.

2. Opening the Module

  1. Sign in to VKVTT using your authorised Google account.
  2. Open Question Bank & Paper Builder from your teaching area.
  3. Your workspace normally contains Add Question, My History, Verified Bank, Paper Builder, and Import / Export. Subject Coordinators also see the review area.

  1. Select the relevant Class and Subject.
  2. Choose the Question Type and enter the Marks.
  3. Type, paste or dictate the question.
  4. Add academic metadata only when it is useful.
  5. Review the final wording and then choose Save Draft or Submit to Subject Coordinator.
Always visually check specialised text. Mathematical expressions, Assamese/Sanskrit/Hindi/Bengali spelling, scientific symbols, names, dates and numerical values should be checked before submission.

4. Voice Dictation

The question field includes a microphone-based dictation helper.

  1. Tap 🎙 Dictate Question.
  2. Select the required language: English (India), Assamese, Hindi or Bengali.
  3. Speak clearly. Recognised speech is added to the text already present; it does not automatically submit anything.
  4. Use Stop when finished.
  5. Review the transcription carefully before submission.

Simple helpers such as Tidy spacing, Capitalise and Add full stop may be used after dictation. These are intended only for basic presentation and punctuation, not for silently changing the academic meaning.

Browser note: Speech recognition depends on the browser/device. If dictation is unavailable or permission is denied, type or paste the question normally.

5. Guidance Callouts and Submission Sound

The interface now contains short speech-bubble style callouts that guide and encourage teachers while they work. They may remind you about the minimum fields, suggest using dictation, or indicate that a question is ready for submission.

After a successful submission, the interface may play a brief motivating confirmation tone. The sound is only a success cue; it does not affect the saved question or workflow.

6. Draft vs Submit

Save Draft keeps incomplete or unfinished work in your own Question Bank history. Drafts are not sent for verification.

Submit to Subject Coordinator places the question into the academic verification workflow. A submitted question remains visible in your history while the coordinator reviews it.

7. Status Meanings

Draft Teacher-only working record.

Awaiting Verification Submitted and waiting for Subject Coordinator review.

Returned for Correction Coordinator has sent it back with a note.

Verified Academically accepted and available in the verified Question Bank.

8. My Submission History

The History area is the teacher's record of contribution and workflow.

  1. Use the quick filters for All, Verified, Awaiting Verification, Returned and Drafts.
  2. Use the search box to find a question by question text, class, subject or visible metadata.
  3. Check the live result count to see how many records match your filter.
  4. For returned questions, read the coordinator's correction note carefully before making changes.

9. Verified Question Bank

The shared Verified Bank contains coordinator-accepted questions. Use it while preparing teaching material or building a paper. Verified questions provide a stronger academic starting point because they have already gone through subject review.

Quality over repetition: Avoid repeated or near-duplicate submissions merely to increase counts. Useful, accurate and reusable questions are more valuable to the school.

10. Subject Coordinator Review Inbox

If you are a Subject Coordinator, a dedicated review area appears for your delegated subject(s).

  1. Review the teacher name, class, subject, question, marks, type and available metadata.
  2. Use the inbox search to find pending questions quickly.
  3. Select ✓ Verify & Accept only after checking academic correctness, wording and appropriateness.
  4. Select ↩ Return when correction is needed and enter a useful correction note.
  5. The teacher then sees the returned status and note in My History.

Coordinator authority is a delegated responsibility; it does not replace the person's normal teacher workspace.

11. Principal / Admin Oversight

The Principal/Admin has a separate oversight area in addition to the teacher workspace.

The current build distinguishes between Submission Contribution and Verified Contribution:

Submission Leaderboard
Counts questions actually sent into the workflow, including awaiting verification, verified and returned questions. Drafts are excluded.
Verified Leaderboard
Counts questions that have passed Subject Coordinator verification.

Principal/Admin views also support institutional monitoring, subject/coordinator management and related Question Bank controls. More detailed qualitative analytics are being developed progressively.

12. Question Paper Builder — Current Build

The Paper Builder is being developed in safe stages. The current build provides a local-first paper-building workspace, so ordinary drafting does not overwrite the Question Bank or publish anything institutionally.

  1. Create a paper and enter the target marks.
  2. Add and rename Sections.
  3. Add section-specific instructions where required.
  4. Add questions under the appropriate section.
  5. Add an optional internal choice (OR) to a normal question where needed.
  6. Watch the live marks indicator to see whether the paper is under target, exactly on target, or over target.
  7. Remove an entire section only after confirming the warning.
Current-stage limitation: The Paper Builder is still being matured. Verified-bank insertion, richer automatic numbering/sub-question logic, final print layout, blueprint analysis and server-side named/versioned paper storage are being added progressively. Do not assume a feature is active merely because it appears in the long-term roadmap.

13. Internal Choice and Marks

When an internal choice is used, the alternative question should represent the same intended marks for that choice. The live paper total should reflect the marks actually expected from the student, not simply add both alternatives as compulsory questions.

Always check the final marks total manually before treating a paper as ready.

14. Import / Export

Import

  1. Open Import / Export.
  2. Download the provided Excel template when importing teacher Question Bank data.
  3. Keep the supplied headings unchanged.
  4. Review imported questions after import rather than assuming spreadsheet content is automatically correct.

Export

Use Export My Questions to download your own Question Bank records. Principal/Admin reporting is handled separately.

15. Good Question-Bank Practice

  1. Write questions that are clear, answerable and appropriate for the class level.
  2. Choose marks that match the expected depth of response.
  3. Use difficulty thoughtfully rather than labelling most questions the same way.
  4. Add Learning Outcome / Competency information when it genuinely helps future reuse.
  5. Include a useful answer or marking scheme for questions where evaluation may otherwise be ambiguous.
  6. Avoid duplicates and cosmetic variations of the same question.
  7. Do not depend blindly on dictation or correction helpers; the submitting teacher remains responsible for the final question.
Remember: The Question Bank is not just a counting system. Every good verified question becomes a reusable academic resource for future papers, revision, practice and school-level planning.

16. What Is Still Being Improved?

The present build is already usable, but development is continuing carefully. Upcoming improvements include deeper coordinator suggestion/correction handling, richer Principal teacher/subject/class analytics, verified-bank insertion into papers, improved paper numbering and sub-question structures, print-ready layouts, answer-key support, paper health/blueprint checks, versioned paper storage and qualitative contribution indicators.

Safety principle: New Question Bank and Paper Builder capabilities are being added incrementally so that the working production system, existing questions and verification history are preserved.

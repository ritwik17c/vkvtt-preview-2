Question Bank & Paper Builder

Teacher User Guide · Vivekananda Kendra Vidyalaya, Nalbari · VKVTT
Core workflow: Teacher creates → saves draft → submits → Subject Coordinator verifies → verified question enters the Question Bank → verified count contributes to the Principal's leaderboard.

1. Opening the Module

  1. Sign in to VKVTT using your authorised Google account.
  2. In My Area / Section 2, open Question Bank & Paper Builder.
  3. The tile is shown only to linked teaching staff. Non-teaching staff cannot access this module.

2. Adding a Question

  1. Open Add Question.
  2. Choose the relevant class, subject and question fields.
  3. Type or paste the question carefully.
  4. Verify every field before saving or submitting.
  5. Add the answer/expected answer and marking scheme where appropriate.
Important: Never submit a mathematical expression, Sanskrit passage, Assamese spelling or scientific symbol without visually checking the final text.

3. Draft vs Submit

Save Draft keeps the question in your personal working history. Submit to Subject Coordinator sends it for academic verification. Only verified questions enter the shared approved Question Bank and count towards verified-question contribution.

4. If a Question Is Returned

  1. Open My Submission History.
  2. Read the Subject Coordinator's correction note.
  3. Correct the question.
  4. Resubmit it for verification.

The workflow trail should remain available for institutional review.

5. My Submission History

Use the history view to follow drafts, submitted questions, returned questions and verified questions.

6. Verified Question Bank

Use class, subject and other filters to find suitable verified questions. The shared bank contains coordinator-verified questions only.

7. Subject Coordinators

If you are assigned as a Subject Coordinator, the review area appears. Verify academic correctness, wording, marks/difficulty, answer and marking scheme. Accept only after checking the question; return it with a clear correction note when needed.

8. Importing Questions

  1. Open Import / Export.
  2. Download the Excel import template.
  3. Keep the given column headings unchanged.
  4. Fill the required question details.
  5. Import the Excel file and review the imported records before submission.

9. Exporting

Use Export My Questions to download your question records. The Principal/Admin has separate institutional reports and oversight.

10. Status Meanings

Draft — not submitted. Pending Review — with Subject Coordinator. Returned — correction required. Verified — accepted into the approved Question Bank and counted as a verified contribution.

Remember: Quality matters. The verified-question leaderboard is intended to recognise useful, academically sound contributions—not repeated or near-duplicate questions.

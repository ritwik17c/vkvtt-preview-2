Teachers may correct or withdraw their own Unverified/Returned submissions. A Returned question can be corrected and resubmitted for verification. Subject Coordinators automatically self-verify their own questions for subjects assigned to them; they do not need to send those questions back to their own Coordinator Inbox. The Coordinator Inbox remains for reviewing other teachers’ submissions in assigned subjects. One coordinator may hold several subjects; the same inbox combines them. Verified questions remain locked. While composing a new question, this device keeps a local safety copy.
